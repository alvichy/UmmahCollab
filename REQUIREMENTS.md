# **Ummah Collaborative Platform**

*Version 0.1 – Draft for Team Feedback*
*Date: 2025-08-09*

---

## **1. Overview**

The platform will enable the Ummah to act as an online community to **share ideas, collaborate, and match the right people together**.

* **Subject-matter experts** (e.g., historians, scholars, dawah experts) can be consulted.
* **Ideas** can be shared, discovered, and ranked.
* **Tech experts** can find and join projects to work on.

---

## **2. Goals**

* Facilitate discovery of **people** and **projects**.
* Provide a structured way of **creating and managing projects**.
* Enable effective **distribution of relevant ideas**.
* Allow **multi-role collaboration** within projects.
* Build a **verified expert directory**.
* Encourage **knowledge sharing**.
* Preserve and store **current knowledge** for future reference.

---

## **3. Features**

### 3.1 Showcasing Ideas

**Purpose:** A place to share early-stage ideas, actions, and requests for help.

* Post **ideas** with title, description, images/links, and hashtags.
* Post **showcases** of completed work or milestones.
* Tag collaborators and link to related projects.
* Clickable hashtags for discovery.
* Ability to **upvote/downvote** ideas.
* Filter feed by **Ideas**, **Showcases**, or **Projects**.

---

### 3.2 Projects

**Purpose:** Structure early-stage ideas into formal collaborations with milestones and defined goals.

* Create a **project listing**.
* Define the **roles needed** (skills, availability, expertise).
* Ideation / Revision of the idea.
* Detect **overlap** with existing ideas.
* Public project page with **progress updates**.
* Describe **benefits and intended outcomes**.

---

### 3.3 Expert Directory

**Purpose:** Keep ideas grounded in correct knowledge by consulting relevant experts.

* Allow experts to **sign up** and share availability.
* Optional **credential verification** process.
* **Booking system** for consultations (MVP: free; later: optional paid).

---

### 3.4 Matching & Discovery

**Purpose:** Ensure the right people connect with each other and the right projects.

* Search for people by **skillset**, **time commitment**, **speciality**.
* Discover **trending projects** and hashtags.
